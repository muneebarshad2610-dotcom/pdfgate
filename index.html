How to Play

Three Moves
and You’re In

Getting a season started takes about thirty seconds. The hard part is lasting to the final round.

1

Add the Bot

Invite House of Games to your server with one click. No configuration, no channels to set up.

2

Gather Your Crew

Players use /join to enter the lobby. The host starts the game once ten players are in.

3

Play the Season

Campaign mode eliminates players round by round. Standalone and local modes let you play without the stakes.

Your Season, Your Way

Three Ways
to Play

Whether you want a full tournament or a quick warm-up, there’s a mode for it.

Campaign

A full season with eliminations. Players are voted out round by round until one champion remains. Persistent leaderboards track every victory across your server.

Standalone

Jump into any game without commitment. Scores reset each session and there’re no eliminations. Perfect for casual rounds or introducing new players.

Local

Practice mode with no tracking. Learn the rules, test strategies, play for fun. Nothing gets recorded — pure sandbox.
